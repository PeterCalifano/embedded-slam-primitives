# Provenance

The design selectively re-expresses the track-management concepts of Pietro Califano's slam-primitives and retains the packaging conventions of cpp_cuda_template_project. Inspected baselines and source hashes are in docs/source-manifest.json. The upstream MIT license is retained unmodified. New C code and support files were prepared for this project with AI assistance and require independent review. This delivery does not claim safety certification or MISRA compliance.
